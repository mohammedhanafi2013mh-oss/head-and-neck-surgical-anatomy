    ↑ ↓ to move · Enter to open · Esc to close

    Quick guide

    • Rotate by dragging, zoom with the wheel or a pinch, and pan with shift-drag, right-drag or two fingers.
    • Tap a structure to read about it. Double-tap it to hide it; "Show hidden" brings it back.
    • Turn whole layers on and off with the chips above the model, and choose how many labels to show.
    • Trace a course at the top of Structures follows a nerve, vessel or route of spread from origin to branches.
    • Surgery walks through an operation step by step; CT / MRI slices the model and shows real Visible Human scans.
    • Search (/) finds any structure in any region. Links to a structure can be shared: the address bar always points to what you are looking at.
