Privacy policy

Head & Neck Surgical Anatomy · last updated October 2026

Head & Neck Surgical Anatomy is an educational app. It does not ask for, collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information, and it has no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

The app remembers a few settings in your device's own storage, so they are there next time you open it:

  • your choice of theme and label style, and whether you have seen the first-use tip;
  • your quiz progress, bookmarks and recently viewed structures;
  • copies of the app's own files (3D models, images and text), so it works offline.

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Network use

The app downloads its own content (3D models, scans and text) from the internet the first time a region is opened, unless it is already on the device. These requests carry no personal information beyond what any web request does (such as your IP address, which the hosting service, GitHub Pages, may log under GitHub's privacy statement).

Links to references, licences or "Report an error" open in your web browser and leave the app; what you do on those sites is covered by their own policies. Reporting an error uses GitHub and needs a GitHub account; nothing is sent unless you choose to submit the report.

Permissions

The Android app uses internet access only, to download its content. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

The app is intended for medical students, trainees and clinicians, and is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
