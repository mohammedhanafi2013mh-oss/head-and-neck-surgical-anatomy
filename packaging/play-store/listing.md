## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Interactive 3D surgical anatomy of the head and neck, made for medical students, ENT and maxillofacial trainees, and surgeons revising before a list.

FIFTEEN REGIONS
Face and facial nerve · oral cavity (mouth open, with the palate, tonsillar pillars and tonsils) · submandibular triangle · parotid · lateral neck · thyroid and central neck · larynx · carotid triangle · deep neck spaces · skull base · infratemporal fossa · pterygopalatine fossa · orbit · nose and paranasal sinuses · temporal bone and ear.

EXPLORE IN 3D
• Rotate, zoom and switch layers (bone, muscles, glands, nerves, arteries, veins, lymph nodes) on accurate models, both sides.
• Tap any structure for its relations, supply, surgical relevance, danger points, variations and references.
• Names of other structures in the notes are links: tap to see them together on the model.
• Trace nerves and vessels along their course: the facial nerve through the temporal bone and parotid, the recurrent laryngeal nerves, the accessory nerve, the lingual nerve and more.

SURGICAL WALKTHROUGHS
Sixteen operations step by step, including parotidectomy, thyroidectomy, neck dissection, laryngectomy, carotid endarterectomy, FESS, the endoscopic approach to the pituitary, glossectomy, ranula excision and cortical mastoidectomy. Incisions are drawn on the model, and the structures at risk in each operation are highlighted with what injuring them causes.

LEARN ACTIVELY
• Dissect layer by layer, from superficial to deep, with a quick check at each layer.
• Quizzes: multiple-choice questions, "Find it on the model" and "Name it". Structures you miss come back more often; progress is saved on your device.

CROSS-SECTIONS
Slice the model in any plane and compare with real CT and MRI of the Visible Human, labelled from the registered model.

ALSO
Original diagrams for every region · search across all regions · bookmarks and recently viewed · light and dark themes · works fully offline · no account, adverts or tracking.

For education only. Check every surgical detail against current atlases and senior guidance before using it in clinical practice.

3D models: Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0), based on BodyParts3D. CT and MRI: Visible Human Project, courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine.
